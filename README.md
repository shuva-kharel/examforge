# 📝 ExamForge

> An AI-powered exam preparation platform that generates dynamic question papers, helping students develop critical thinking instead of memorizing static question banks.

---

## 📖 Overview

Traditional question banks expose students to the same questions repeatedly, encouraging memorization rather than understanding. ExamForge solves this by generating unique practice papers where questions are intelligently paraphrased while preserving the original concept and difficulty.

Every practice session feels like a new exam, allowing students to focus on reasoning, comprehension, and problem-solving.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered question generation using Google Gemini

## 🧩 Problem Statement

Students often rely on static question banks where the same questions appear repeatedly. This encourages memorization instead of understanding concepts, resulting in poor critical thinking and weaker problem-solving abilities during real examinations.

---

## 💡 Solution

ExamForge leverages AI to dynamically generate and paraphrase exam questions while preserving their underlying concepts and difficulty. Students receive a fresh set of questions every time they practice, forcing them to apply knowledge rather than recall memorized answers. This creates a more realistic exam experience and significantly improves conceptual understanding.
